# CP001 — Open Questions and Next Learning Actions

## Highest-value tests

1. **Delayed reconstruction (L2):** Without showing prior labels or diagrams, can the learner reconstruct why open development might outperform closed development, including the three mechanisms and their counterforces?
2. **Exploration vs confirmation:** Given a researcher who tried many variants and reported one successful run with a confidence interval, can the learner identify why the claim is not confirmatory and design a fresh test?

## Additional unresolved tests

- Can the learner separate each arrow in a causal chain such as segmentation -> clinical decision -> patient outcome -> cost effectiveness -> social welfare, and attach evidence/uncertainty to each?
- Can the learner state when reputation is legitimate contextual evidence for allocating attention versus when it would improperly contaminate interpretation of experimental results?
- Can the learner express scaling extrapolation with a tested range, uncertainty, and explicit conditions rather than "assume the trend holds"?
- Can the learner distinguish hypothesis, experiment, observation/evidence, finding, and decision under ambiguous examples?
- Can a later comparator test whether judgment-first learning actually improves durable transfer relative to explanation-first learning after accounting for effort?
