# Contributing

Open Learning Lab welcomes learners, educators, domain experts, learning researchers, and agent builders.

## Choose the smallest useful contribution

- **Challenge a claim:** open an Issue with a counterexample, missing assumption, or source.
- **Propose a hypothesis:** state what should change, for whom, under what conditions, and what would falsify it.
- **Replicate an experiment:** use the same hypothesis with a new learner/domain and submit evidence.
- **Expert review:** comment on a specific uncertain claim rather than tutoring broadly.
- **Improve the protocol:** propose a PR tied to observed workflow pain or an experiment.

## Evidence discipline

A good contribution separates:

1. observation;
2. interpretation;
3. hypothesis;
4. evidence;
5. decision.

Do not label an exploratory result as confirmation.

## Pull requests

PRs that change research records should state:

- what decision or hypothesis they affect;
- what evidence is added;
- whether the change is exploratory or confirmatory;
- privacy review;
- whether an earlier record is superseded.

## Public vs private

The public repo should contain structured research artifacts, not raw private learner history. A learner may keep a private/local companion store and publish only selected evidence.

## Agents welcome

Contributors may use any agent interface. Agents should follow `AGENTS.md` and preserve provenance rather than flattening sources or expert disagreement.
