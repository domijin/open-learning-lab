## Emerging

- **Claim-centric research memory:** The learner proposed that durable progress should capture falsifiable hypotheses, effect magnitude, uncertainty, and boundary conditions rather than benchmark wins alone.
- **Exploration vs confirmation:** The assistant introduced the distinction after the learner proposed confidence intervals; the learner has not yet reconstructed or transferred this distinction.
- **Causal-boundary discipline:** The learner naturally builds multi-step causal/system stories. The assistant separated MRI segmentation -> clinical decision -> patient outcome -> cost effectiveness -> social welfare into distinct claims, but that correction has not yet been tested.
- **Reputation in governance:** The learner proposed reputation as contextual input for proposal evaluation. It remains untested whether reputation will be kept to triage/attention rather than evidence interpretation.

## Misconceptions / contradictions

- **Scaling extrapolation is underqualified:** The learner said one can test cheaply and "assume the scaling trend would hold." This is a practical extrapolation strategy, but the assumption requires an explicit tested range, boundary conditions, and uncertainty; this correction has not yet been retrieved.
- **Causal-chain compression:** In the MRI transfer, downstream clinical/economic/welfare links were narrated together before each arrow had evidence. This is a reasoning risk, not a demonstrated persistent misconception.
- **Prior-exposure contamination:** The initial assistant walkthrough described Marin's Speedrun, multi-scale testing, negative results, and open-development framing before the judgment-first sequence. V001 and related rediscovery observations should therefore be interpreted as partially exposed in-session reasoning, not fully blind independent invention. Earlier evidence files remain unchanged.
