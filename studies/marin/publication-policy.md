# Marin Study Publication Policy

## Scope

For Study 001 (Marin), the learner has opted to make **study-relevant learning progress and Marin-related learning details public by default** so the learning process can be inspected, challenged, and reused as part of Open Learning Lab.

This is a study-specific override of the repository's more conservative default privacy posture.

## May be published

- learner-authored reasoning about Marin;
- misconceptions, corrections, uncertainty, and changes of mind relevant to the study;
- selected verbatim learner excerpts;
- normalized versions of voice-to-text excerpts when clearly labeled;
- retrieval and transfer-test results;
- study-relevant learner-state interpretations;
- Marin sources, source maps, and current-project updates;
- learning interventions and prompts;
- evidence and findings derived from the study;
- public expert feedback related to the study, with appropriate attribution/consent.

## Do not publish automatically

- unrelated personal information;
- hidden model memory or hidden system context;
- unrelated prior conversations;
- credentials, account identifiers, or secrets;
- sensitive personal information not necessary for the study;
- private third-party communications without consent;
- anything the learner explicitly marks private.

## Provenance rule

When preserving a learner utterance:

1. keep **verbatim** wording when scientifically useful;
2. optionally add a **normalized** version for obvious voice-to-text errors;
3. never silently replace the historical wording;
4. keep interpretation separate from observation.

## Third-party content

Public permission from the learner does not change the license of Marin or other third-party material. Prefer links, short attributed excerpts where appropriate, and paraphrase. Preserve original source/license information.

## Revocation and mistakes

Git publication is durable. If sensitive material is accidentally committed, privacy takes precedence over append-only research provenance and repository history may be rewritten when necessary.
