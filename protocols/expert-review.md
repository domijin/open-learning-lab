# Protocol — Expert Review

Route a narrow, high-value uncertainty to an expert.

Provide:
- learner goal;
- target claim;
- current evidence;
- current interpretation;
- specific question.

Ask the expert for:
- support/challenge/alternative;
- rationale or source;
- expertise context;
- confidence if meaningful;
- what evidence would change their judgment.

Do not ask experts to restate textbook material unless that is itself the experiment.

Preserve disagreement rather than generating false consensus.
