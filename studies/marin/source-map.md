## Retrieval rule

Before teaching a claim that may have changed—project status, architecture, current experiments, recent results—the research agent should retrieve the current primary source and record the date.

## Learning-material rule

Do not turn this list into a fixed reading queue. Choose sources based on the learner's current conceptual gap and active hypothesis.
