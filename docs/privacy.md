# Privacy

Open learning does not require open private life.

## Default rule

**Private learner data stays private unless the learner explicitly chooses to publish a minimal research artifact.**

Do not commit:
- raw private chat histories by default;
- credentials or account identifiers;
- health, financial, legal, or other sensitive personal data;
- hidden system/model context;
- third-party private messages without consent.

## Publish the minimum sufficient evidence

Prefer:
- a structured test result;
- a learner-authored concept summary;
- a selected excerpt directly relevant to the hypothesis;
- aggregate time/effort;
- an anonymized or pseudonymized replication.

Avoid publishing an entire transcript when one observation suffices.

## Expert consent

Attribute public expert judgments only with consent. Otherwise record an anonymized role/context when scientifically necessary and permissible.

## Local companion state

Personalized agents may maintain richer private learner state locally or in a private store. The public repository should be able to function without it.

## Deletion and correction

Git is difficult to erase reliably after publication. Treat publication as durable. Sensitive information accidentally committed should be removed promptly and may require history rewriting despite the research ledger's normal append-only convention; privacy overrides provenance.
